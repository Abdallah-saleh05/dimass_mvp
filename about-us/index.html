Our company

Overview

Dimass is a general contracting company specializing in commercial construction and real estate investment. Established in April 2011, the company undertakes concrete and steel construction, fuel station works, roads and sanitation projects.

The company’s profile documents work for clients in energy, manufacturing and construction. Its project archive covers building renewal, industrial facilities, fuel station construction and associated infrastructure.

Dimass describes a management approach based on defined policies, project requirements and safe working practices, with an ongoing focus on satisfying clients.
