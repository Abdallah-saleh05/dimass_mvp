Health & safety

SAFE WORKING PRACTICES START WITH OUR PEOPLE

Working together.
Working safely.

Dimass describes safety as a structured part of its working approach. The company profile sets out the commitments below for construction site activities.

Safety recognition certificates are included in the company profile. They are historical records and do not imply current certification or a stated accident-free hours total.

View safety recognition
01

Assess the risks

Analyze potential risks across the project and establish initial measures to address them.

02

Plan waste disposal

Prepare a project waste disposal plan to minimize the risks associated with site waste.

03

Protect our people

Provide the personal protective equipment needed to protect workers on site.

04

Review changing conditions

Update risk assessments as conditions and activities change in the work area.

05

Build safety awareness

Hold regular worker meetings about safe working practices and site safety rules.
